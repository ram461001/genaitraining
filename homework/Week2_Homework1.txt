Homework 1 — Build a RAG System on the Bhagavad Gita or Bible
==============================================================

WHAT YOU WILL BUILD
--------------------
A question-answering system that can answer questions like:
  - "What does the Gita say about duty?"
  - "What does the Bible say about forgiveness?"

Using only the actual text of the book — not the LLM's general knowledge.


PREREQUISITES (Before You Start)
----------------------------------
- Ollama running locally with llama3.1 pulled
- Completed notebooks 2.1 through 2.6
- Download the PDF of your chosen book (Gita or Bible) and place it in your Week2/ folder


STEP-BY-STEP INSTRUCTIONS
--------------------------

Step 1 — Load the Book
  - Use PyPDFLoader from LangChain to load the PDF
  - Print how many pages were loaded
  - Print the first page to make sure it loaded correctly

Step 2 — Chunk the Text
  - Use RecursiveCharacterTextSplitter to split the text into smaller pieces
  - Try chunk_size=500 and chunk_overlap=100
  - Print how many chunks were created
  - Print 2-3 sample chunks and observe what they look like

Step 3 — Create Embeddings & Vector Store
  - Use OllamaEmbeddings with llama3.1
  - Store the chunks in a Chroma vector store
  - Save it to disk with persist_directory so you don't re-embed every time

Step 4 — Build the RAG Chain
  - Create a prompt that tells the LLM:
      "You are a scholar of [Gita/Bible]. Answer only from the given context."
      "If the answer is not in the context, say 'This is not mentioned in the text.'"
  - Wire it together: retriever | prompt | llm

Step 5 — Ask At Least 5 Questions
  Test your system with meaningful questions.

  For Gita:
    1. What does Krishna say about action without attachment?
    2. What is the nature of the soul according to the Gita?
    3. What does the Gita say about a person of steady wisdom?
    4. What are the three modes of nature?
    5. What does Krishna say about those who worship other gods?

  For Bible:
    1. What does Jesus say about loving your enemies?
    2. What is the meaning of the parable of the prodigal son?
    3. What does the Bible say about faith and works?
    4. How should a person treat the poor?
    5. What does the Bible say about forgiveness?

Step 6 — Compare RAG vs No RAG
  - Ask the same question WITH and WITHOUT the RAG chain
  - Print both answers side by side
  - Write a short note: What was different? Which answer was more grounded?

Step 7 — Reflection (Write in a Markdown cell)
  Answer these in your notebook:
    1. What chunk size worked best and why?
    2. Did the retriever ever return irrelevant chunks? Give an example.
    3. What would break this system? What are its limitations?


BONUS CHALLENGES (Optional)
-----------------------------
  MMR Retrieval   -> Use search_type='mmr' to reduce redundant chunks
  Metadata        -> Add chapter or page number to each chunk's metadata and display it in the answer
  Conversational  -> Make it remember previous questions (use ConversationBufferMemory)
  Evaluation      -> For 3 questions, manually check if the retrieved chunks actually contained the answer

A Jupyter notebook (.ipynb) with:

  - The reflection markdown cell
